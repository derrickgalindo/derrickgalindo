How Roblox hits happen, and what to build first

A follow-up to the main report. It uses live data from Roblox's own APIs, Roblox's Creator Hub documentation on discovery, full player-count histories for every game launched since July, and press reports on studio acquisitions.

  1. New games reach the top fast.37 of today's top 100 were created in the last 120 days. Steal An Egg, today's #1, launched July 25, reached 1M concurrent players on day 28, and peaked around day 56.
  2. Every launch follows the same shape: a wait, a jump, a peak, a fall.New games take a median of 19 days to reach 1,000 players. The recommender then lifts them to 10,000 in about 2.6 days, and they peak around day 30. Three weeks after the peak, daily play is down to about 46%.
  3. Waves beat originals.Most hits ride a format wave ("Steal a ___", "+1 per second", "Anomaly"), and the leader is usually not the first game in it. Roblox demotes near-copies but explicitly rewards "your own spin" on a trend.
  4. Retention is invisible from outside, so test on Roblox itself.Likes, favorites, server size and game passes don't predict which new games keep their players. Only Roblox's analytics show the signals that decide growth.
  5. The plan: ship a cheap "Anomaly" game to learn the funnel, then build Hide as a Painting v2.The hide-by-blending idea has already drawn up to 60k players on Roblox, but both earlier versions lost nearly everyone. Version 2 adds the collect-and-trade economy that has kept Murder Mystery 2 alive for 12 years.

01 · The basics

How a Roblox game gets made and paid

  • Tools: the free Roblox Studio editor (Windows and Mac). Code is in Luau, Roblox's typed Lua. Engineers usually add Rojo so they can work in VS Code with git.
  • Roblox runs everything else: servers, matchmaking, payments, chat and voice, on phone, PC, console and tablet. You pay nothing to host.
  • Money: players buy Robux and spend it on game passes (one-time unlocks) and developer products (repeat purchases). Roblox adds engagement-based Creator Rewards, and you convert Robux to dollars through its payout program (DevEx). Creators received 22% of all Roblox bookings in 2025.
  • Adults pay more: since June 8, 2026, spending by age-checked US adults in games using R15 avatars earns a 42% higher payout rate. That group spends over 50% more than under-18 players.
  • Who owns the hits: 94 of today's top 100 games belong to groups (studios or teams), not individual accounts.

02 · From Roblox's own Creator Hub docs

What the recommendation feed rewards

The Home "Recommended For You" feed ranks games on these signals:

  • Play-through rate: how often people who see the game play it.
  • First-play bounce: players who leave within 60 seconds or 61–180 seconds. This counts against you.
  • Return days: play days per user on day 1, days 2–7 and days 8–28.
  • Playtime: capped at 60 minutes per player per day.
  • Co-play: days players come back to play with friends.
  • Engagement and spending: qualified sessions, spend days and Robux spent per user.

Only recommended players count

"Roblox doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads, curation, friends, search, social media, or any other source." Outside traffic only speeds up your chance of being tested.

Explore, then expand

Roblox tests a game on one group of users and expands to similar groups if they engage. Evaluation starts from the first session, and a weak start isn't penalized.

Copies get demoted

Games whose "metadata and place files closely resemble existing games" are no longer prioritized. The docs explicitly say to "add your own spin to existing trends".

A custom thumbnail is required

Games with only a default thumbnail get no home-page impressions at all.

Who's on top right now

Only 10 games are at or above ~75,000 concurrent, which I use as the working equivalent of 1M daily players. The real figure is anywhere from 40k to 150k, depending on how long players stay each day.

05 · Copying, done right

Format waves drive the charts, and the winner is rarely the first game

Other waves running right now:

  • "+1 per second" incrementals: 18 games, 12 launched in the last 120 days.
  • "Anomaly" shift-work horror: 9 games, 6 new. Sell Fries! [Anomaly] went from launch to 105k concurrent in 26 days.

Roblox demotes near-copies, so the play is a recognizable format with a real twist.

06 · What holds players

Nothing you can see from outside predicts retention

What the long-lived games share (Murder Mystery 2, Adopt Me, Blox Fruits, Fisch, Pet Simulator 99):

  • A loop players repeat: rounds, collection or progression.
  • A collection or trading economy: Murder Mystery 2's knives, Adopt Me's pets, Fish It's "1,000,000+ variations".
  • Rare-roll mutations and earnings while offline.
  • Seasonal events.
  • Server sizes that fit the loop: about 8 per server for tycoon and steal games, about 24 for survival, about 30 for roleplay.

07 · Revenue and exits

A real market for Roblox games, at 1–12 months of revenue

  • Revenue per player: RoWatcher's revenue model puts top games at about $0.3–0.5 a day per concurrent player. At 60 minutes of play a day, that's roughly $0.01–0.02 per daily player, in line with the main report's $0.012 mid estimate. These are estimates; Roblox doesn't publish per-game revenue.
  • An active buyers' market: Bloomberg (Jul 2025) reported a game-buying frenzy after Roblox made ownership transfers easier in December 2024. Reports disagree on whether 7 or 15 of June 2025's top earners had changed owners. Buyers include Do Big Studios, Voldex, Splitting Point and Embracer.
  • Prices: reportedly 1–12 months of in-game revenue, brokered on Discord. Blue Lock: Rivals sold for more than $3M while earning about $5M a month, after roughly three months of work by a 19-year-old.
  • Studio hits: Steal An Egg came from Misfits Gaming's portfolio of Roblox games.

Sell or keep, updated

A fad-shaped hit delivers about one month of peak-level play over its whole run, so a sale or partner stake near the peak usually beats holding it. Keep it and invest in live updates only if it holds: above about 40% of peak three weeks after peaking.

08 · Concepts, re-scored on Roblox's terms

Hide as a Painting v2 leads, with a cheap Anomaly game to learn the ropes first

This is an evidence rubric, not a fitted model, because public data didn't predict retention. Each criterion scores 0–2:

  • Pull: how far the closest recent copy got.
  • Crowding: how many same-format games launched in the last 120 days (fewer scores higher).
  • Retention engine: collection, trading or progression.
  • Co-play, watchability, solo build risk and moderation risk.

The closest existing games to our concepts

The blend-in-and-hide mechanic draws players: Paint Or Seek peaked at 60k, close to 1M-DAU scale. Both earlier versions then lost almost everyone, and their like ratios are weak. Version 2 copies what has kept Murder Mystery 2 alive for 12 years: short social rounds plus collecting and trading cosmetics (frames, paints, poses), with the museum changing for each event.

Revised plan

  1. Drop the web test. On Roblox, growth depends on play-through rate, bounce, D1/D7 and co-play, all of which Creator Analytics measures. Group-chat sharing doesn't move Roblox discovery.
  2. Weeks 1–3: Night-Shift Museum [Anomaly]. A spot-the-anomaly co-op shift as a museum guard. It's cheap, rides a rising wave and is very watchable. Treat it as disposable: its job is to teach you thumbnails, play-through rate and D1, and to produce museum assets you'll reuse.
  3. Weeks 3–10: Hide as a Painting v2, on the same assets, with the collect-and-trade economy built in from day one.
  4. Kill rule: give each launch 4–5 weeks and at least two updates, since the median wait to 1k is 19 days. Kill it if it never passes about 1k concurrent and Creator Analytics shows it in the bottom half of similar games. Scale it if it clears 10k.
  5. If it breaks out: sell or take a partner near the peak if the curve looks like a fad. Keep it and run live updates if it holds.

09 · Limits and sources

What to keep in mind

  • The sample is games visible today, so the climb odds are optimistic.
  • RoWatcher's histories start June 30, 2026 and sample about every 4 hours, which understates peaks. Its revenue figures are model estimates.
  • The 75k-concurrent ≈ 1M DAU equivalence depends on how long players stay each day.
  • The 3-hourly chart snapshots only run while this cloud session is alive.
