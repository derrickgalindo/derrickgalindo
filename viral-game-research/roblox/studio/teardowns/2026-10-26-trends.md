# Trend note: 2026-10-26

*Trend scout, Mon Oct 26. Data: `data/roblox_games.csv` (Roblox API, pulled ~13:50 UTC today) and RoWatcher player histories in `data/raw/rowatcher/` (samples every ~4 h, latest 12:00 UTC today). "Same-hour WoW" compares the 12:00 UTC sample today with the one at 12:00 UTC last Monday, so time of day and weekday match. RoWatcher revenue numbers are model estimates.*

## The 3 things that matter
1. **Sell Fries! did not rebound. The "24k → 52k" jump comes from the time of day each snapshot was taken.**
   - Oct 22's snapshot was taken around 02 UTC, Sell Fries' daily low (25.7k–27.2k that night). Today's was taken around 13 UTC, its daily high.
   - At the same hour it is down 5% on last week: 54.6k last Monday at 14 UTC vs 52.2k today at 12 UTC.
   - Saturday's high was 87k, down from 105k the Saturday before (−17%).
   - I found no update in the RoWatcher change log this week. The only new content I can see is Halloween codes that pay out candies ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/sell-fries-codes/), a third party).
   - It is following the median curve: it peaked on day 26 and is 9 days past that peak.
   - **The same time-of-day bias affects every "playing now" comparison in `analysis/weekly/2026-10-26.md`.**
2. **The Anomaly wave is holding steady, and new entries keep breaking 10k.**
   - Combined players in the 7 Anomaly games: up 5% at the same hour, while the sum of each game's weekly high is down 1%.
   - Two young games are still climbing:
     - **Clone Baby Care (Anomaly)**, 17 days old, peaked at 31.6k on Saturday;
     - **Airport Security (Anomaly)**, a night-shift inspection game and the closest analog to Night-Shift Museum. Its daily high has risen every day for 13 days, reaching 13.2k yesterday. It has no game passes, and up to 5 friends can play together.
3. **The hide-in-plain-sight mechanic is succeeding inside the egg-steal format, while the plain hide-round games fade.**
   - **Fake an Egg** (you disguise yourself as an egg and steal pets) is up 2.3× at the same hour. Its peak is 80k, though that is a single Sunday sample.
   - **Hide In Pixels** has fallen to 9% of its peak, 15 days after peaking. Titles about hiding or painting are down 19% at the same hour, and the weekly high of the round-based hide games is down 21%.
   - Older 1-vs-All games surged for Halloween:
     - Murder Mystery 2 reached 2.19M on Sunday at 18 UTC, then 329k by 01 UTC as an event apparently ended (cause not confirmed);
     - Violence District hit 188k on Saturday.
   - So the mechanic and the round format both draw players, but only when there is a collection or economy around them. That is already the plan for Hide as a Painting v2.

## Data warnings (worth fixing in `scripts/`)
- **Days-to-1k is often just the day RoWatcher started tracking a game.**
  - For 121 of 218 launches, the first sample was already ≥1k: Break and Steal, Climb, Bike For Egg, Fake an Egg, Hide In Pixels, DONT SPLASH, and The Hollow (first sample 131.7k).
  - On the 58 games tracked before they reached 1k, 1k→10k takes a median of **4.6 days**, and 17% do it within 2 days. The playbook says 2.6 days and 42%.
  - The 19-day median to 1k is 17.3 days on uncensored games.
- **This week's RoWatcher pull only goes back to about Jul 26.** Peaks set before then are understated in today's CSV:
  - Paint Or Seek: 24k now vs 60k in the Oct 22 pull;
  - Animal Hospital: 515k vs 677k.
  - So now/peak is overstated for older games in `tables.md`.
- **Single 4-hourly samples catch scheduled weekend events.** Steal An Egg, Climb, Bike For Egg, Break and Steal and Fake an Egg all spike on Saturday or Sunday between 09 and 14 UTC, so their "peak" numbers are event spikes.
- `analysis/weekly/2026-10-26.md` repeats its Trending and "Launched in the last 60 days" sections twice.

## Changes to the plan?
**No.** The Anomaly wave is still putting out new 10k+ games (Airport Security is still rising and is the closest analog), so Night-Shift Museum first, then Hide as a Painting, still holds. Fake an Egg backs the v2 idea of putting the hide mechanic inside a collection economy. It doesn't change the order.
