# Teardown: [🎨] Hide In Pixels

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10768026334, by Vyxen (Group), launched Sep 25.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10768026334.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description, which says "inspired by Mecca Chameleon".*
- *It's the closest analog to Hide as a Painting, and it shows what to avoid.*

**Core loop:** each round, paint your character block by block to match a giant pixel-art painting, then freeze; it's 1 vs 1, and whoever blends in best wins, with 2 rounds winning the game.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- A giant pixel painting appears, and you start painting your avatar to match it.
- You freeze and hope you aren't spotted, then see who blended better.
- **Troll tools** (jumpscares, flashbangs, screen shake) break the tension.

## Retention engine
- **Win streaks** (sold at 2x through a pass) and "tons of pixel paintings".
- **Nothing else:** no collection, trading, offline progress or ranked ladder.

## Co-play
- Servers hold 20, but matches are 1 vs 1.
- Free private servers: "Invite your friends… Play with friends for FREE".

  - two "[LIMITED]" troll packs: Glitch 79, Explosion 39;

**Where it is on the curve:** 15 days after its peak it is at 9%, against a 46% benchmark at 3 weeks. Its like ratio of 0.61 is the lowest of this week's teardowns. Its favorites are unusually high: 1.19M on 21.5M visits.

## Why it faded (inferred from the data above)
- **1 vs 1 means one player loses every match.**
- **There's nothing to collect** and nothing that carries over to the next session except a streak.
- **No updates after launch week.**

## What we'd steal
- **Match the scene, then freeze, as the hiding verb (Hide as a Painting).**
  - It pulled 23k with no economy behind it. Keep it.
  - Run it in rounds of one seeker against many hiders, MM2-style, instead of 1v1, so most players aren't losing.
- **Cheap poses and troll items as impulse buys (Hide as a Painting).** Its passes start at 3–79 R$. Poses fit the v2 cosmetics list (frames, paints, poses), and are fine to sell because they're cosmetic.
- **Avoid the cliff:** no collection, and no updates after launch.
