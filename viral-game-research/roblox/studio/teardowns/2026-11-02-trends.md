## The 3 things that matter
1. **The Anomaly wave is fading, and Sell Fries' "[CH2]" hasn't moved players.**
   - Sell Fries is down 25% at the same hour. Its Sunday high was 61.0k, vs 82.7k the Sunday before.
   - I can't find what Chapter 2 contains. There are no patch notes on guide sites, and RoWatcher's change log didn't record the rename.
   - The rename happened between Oct 26 14:20 UTC and today. The last publish was Nov 1 20:09 UTC.
   - On Sunday, its ratio to the week before rose from 0.58 at 12:00 to 0.81 at 15:36, then fell back to 0.62–0.77 on Monday. So far that's a few hours of bump, not a lift.
   - The Anomaly games combined, excluding Animal Hospital, are down 28% at the same hour. Airport Security, our closest analog, is at 31% of its peak 8 days after it, against a benchmark of 83% one week after peak.
   - No Anomaly game launched after Oct 9 has passed 10k (24 days). The earlier pace was a new 10k+ launch every ~9 days.
2. **Throw Food For Animals' 20k → 140k is a real, organic breakout. I found no update behind it.**
   - Its curve is a true measurement: tracked from 15 players. It sat under ~55 players for a month, then took off on Oct 20.
   - Milestones: 1k on day 32.5, 10k on day 35.2, 100k on day 41.5. It peaked at 163k on Halloween Saturday and is still at 86% of that on Monday.
   - No trigger is visible: no rename, no description change, and no new game pass since Sep 30.
   - What is visible is top-tier engagement: a 0.94 like ratio, full 5-player servers, and ~20 minutes per visit (derived). This is the recommender's "expand" step working. [Teardown](2026-11-02-throw-food-for-animals.md).
3. **Hiding works when it is part of a steal/collect economy. Plain hide rounds are flat, except one game that is a direct analog of Hide as a Painting.**
   - **Hide to Steal Eggs** (you hide inside an egg to sneak into other players' plots) is up 3.05× to 11.5k. Its curve is real: tracked from launch, 1k on day 17.6. It is the second hit built on Fake an Egg's mechanic. Fake an Egg itself spiked to 93.5k on Sunday.
   - The 7 plain round-based hide games total 16.7k, 0.92× at the same hour. Hide In Pixels is down to 1.4k and has dropped off the charts.
   - **Hide In Drawings 🎨**, launched Oct 8, rose from 254 to 3.5k (13.7×), with a 6.5k Sunday high and a 0.86 like ratio. It's a 1v1 game: both players hide inside a drawing, the first to spot the other wins, and winnings unlock cosmetics.
   - It's under 10k, so it gets no teardown, but it is the closest thing yet to Hide as a Painting. **Watch it next week.**

## Data warnings (worth fixing in `scripts/`)
- **The dataset only keeps games that are on the charts, so faded games disappear.** 132 games dropped out this week, including Hide In Pixels, Paint Or Seek, Hide and Seek Extreme, Animal Daycare (Anomaly) and It Hears The Water.
  - As a result, cluster sums and `roblox_waves.csv` overstate the survivors.
  - The "first" game in the hide wave also changed from Hide and Seek Extreme to Fly Hide & Seek, only because Hide and Seek Extreme dropped out.
  - Fix: keep tracking every game seen in earlier weeks.
- **There is no Anomaly row in `roblox_waves.csv` or `tables.md` §C.** `clean_name()` strips bracketed and parenthesised text, and "Anomaly" always appears as "[Anomaly]" or "(Anomaly)".
- **RoWatcher's change log misses some renames.** The "[CH2]" (Sell Fries) and "[x2 🏆]" (Animal Hospital) renames aren't logged; the latest entries for those games are Oct 15 and Oct 17. Other games have entries up to today. So `name_changes_90d` undercounts. Compare `roblox_meta.json` week to week instead.
- **The RoWatcher history now starts on Aug 2 (last week it started on Jul 26).** Older peaks shrink further: Animal Hospital's peak is 423k in this pull, vs 515k last week and 677k on Oct 22.
- **Favorites are being inflated.** Raise The Biggest Family has 1.10M favorites on 9.8M visits (113 per 1k new visits this week), and Hide to Steal Eggs 59 per 1k, vs 0.6–2.8 for the other games here. That is probably an in-game favorite prompt (an inference). `fav_per_kvisit` isn't comparable across games.
- **55 games launched in the last 60 days have no value for last week**, because RoWatcher started tracking them after Oct 26. WoW numbers for them are not real growth.
