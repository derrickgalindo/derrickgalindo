## The 3 things that matter
1. **Hide as a Painting: the trigger didn't fire, but a direct analog is at 10k.**
   - **Hide In Drawings** peaked at 6.5k on Nov 1. It's now 1.3k, at 20% of that peak, has dropped off the charts, and was last updated Nov 7. It did not pass 10k.
   - **Paint and Hide! 🎨** ("Paint yourself to blend in!", hiders vs a seeker) passed 10k on Oct 27 and peaked at 19.4k on Oct 31. It's still around 10–11k on weekdays and is 10.0k now.
   - Our dataset missed it until this week. [Teardown](2026-11-16-paint-and-hide.md).
   - Its like ratio is 0.72, the same weak score as every paint-to-hide game before it (Paint Or Seek 0.78, Hide In Pixels 0.60).
   - So the mechanic draws players, but nobody has fixed retention yet. That's the gap v2's collect-and-trade economy is meant to fill.
2. **The Anomaly fade sped up, with one exception.**
   - The 11 Anomaly games (dropped ones included) went from 108k to 57k at the same hour (0.52).
   - Sell Fries is at 15.2k, 15% of its peak 30 days after it. The benchmark is 46% three weeks after peak.
   - The exception is **Cook Pizzas [Anomaly]** (launched Oct 23, solo servers). It went from 100 to 6.9k at the same hour and peaked at 15.4k on Sunday. It's the first Anomaly game launched after Oct 9 to pass 10k. Its take-off on Nov 7 came on the same day it added its first game passes. [Teardown](2026-11-16-cook-pizzas-anomaly.md).
3. **Ragdoll/physics is a real wave, but it churns fast and carries moderation risk.**
   - 27 games with "ragdoll" in the title launched in the last 60 days, and 19 of them passed 10k. The cluster grew from 118k to 218k.
   - The same games are at only 0.47 like-for-like, though: each one peaks within about 10–33 days and drops to 9–34% of peak.
   - The two biggest gore sandboxes, **Hyper Realistic Ragdoll** (196k single-sample peak, Nov 14) and **Ragdoll Physics 🩸** (70k peak), now return "content restricted" from the Roblox games API. Their histories stop on Nov 14 and today 04:48 UTC respectively.

*Also: Throw Food For Animals is at 353k at the same hour (2.51×). Monday same-hour values went 19k → 141k → 236k → 353k over the last three weeks, with ~19 minutes per visit throughout. Guides tie its Saturday spikes to a fixed weekly event. [Update teardown](2026-11-16-throw-food-for-animals-update.md).*

## Where each wave is on the curve

  - The first copies have arrived, which is how a wave starts.
- **Ragdoll/physics: middle of the wave, with fast churn.**
  - Games with a real (fully tracked) curve peak early: Ragdoll Research on day 10.6, Realistic Ragdoll Engine on day 18.7, Ragdoll Sandbox on day 22.5, Ragdoll Soccer on day 32.8.

  - New entries still pass 10k within ~10 days.
- **Anomaly: late.**
  - Sell Fries is 30 days past its peak at 15%, and Clone Baby Care is at 24%. Both are below the benchmark.

- **Steal/egg: late.** Steal An Egg is at 13% of its RoWatcher peak. New entries peak at 20–48k (Steal A Balut, Steal a Superpower, Steal a PC) and are at 24–60% of that within days.

## Data warnings (worth fixing in `scripts/`)
- **Games that disappear can look like data errors.**
  - Hyper Realistic Ragdoll and Ragdoll Physics 🩸 now return "[TITLE UNAVAILABLE]" with `isContentRestricted: true` from `games.roblox.com/v1/games`, and RoWatcher marked the first one delisted at Nov 16 06:00.
  - Both are still in `roblox_games.csv` with a blank `playing_now`. Hyper Realistic Ragdoll's 196k peak still counts in the launch statistics.
  - 22 rows have a blank `playing_now`.
- **Collection gap.** Paint and Hide! was at 14.4k on Nov 2 according to RoWatcher, but it isn't in the Nov 2 dataset, and today it has no chart position either. A 10k+ game in our core niche was invisible to us for at least two weeks.
- **Saturday single-sample spikes distort peaks.**
  - Examples: Throw Food (676k on Nov 7 at 12:00, 912k on Nov 14 at 13:12 UTC), Steal An Egg (2.08M on Nov 14 at 13:xx), Hyper Realistic Ragdoll (196k at 13:12).
  - For Throw Food, guides describe a weekly Saturday event at 9 AM ET (14:00 UTC), lasting ~30 minutes.
  - Effect: `roblox_launches.csv` gives Throw Food a peak of 912k and now/peak of 0.39; its next-highest sample is 478k.
  - Fix: use a daily-median or second-highest peak.
- **Dropouts are getting worse:** 208 games left the dataset in two weeks, including Hide In Drawings, Hide to Steal Eggs, Airport Security, Pasahero and Home Alone: Anomalies. This is the same fix as last time: keep tracking every game seen in earlier weeks.
- **Many curves start mid-climb.** Survive Your Ideas was first tracked at 1.3k on day 27, Ragdoll Soccer had an 8-day gap (Nov 1 → Nov 9), Realistic Ragdoll MMA started at 6.9k and Sandworm Playground at 15k. For these games, days-to-1k are upper bounds.
- **`roblox_waves.csv` and `tables.md` §C still have no Anomaly row** (the `clean_name()` bug), and also **no ragdoll row**: the token list is fixed, and the biggest new wave isn't in it.
- **The 90-day RoWatcher window keeps cutting old peaks.** Paint Or Seek's peak is now 10.6k in RoWatcher, vs 60k in PLAYBOOK.md.

## Changes to the plan?
**No change to the order.**
- **Night-Shift Museum [Anomaly] first:** the wave is late, so it won't carry the game. Cook Pizzas shows that a twist can still reach ~15k in 3 weeks. Keep the 2–3 week budget.
- **Hide as a Painting second:** the trigger (Hide In Drawings passing 10k) didn't fire. Paint and Hide confirms the mechanic draws players and that retention is still unsolved, which is v2's pitch. There's no reason to pull it forward ahead of the learning launch.
- **Ragdoll:** watch it, don't chase it. It's churny, physics-heavy for a solo developer, and two of its leaders were just pulled.
