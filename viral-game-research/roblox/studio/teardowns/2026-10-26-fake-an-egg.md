# Teardown: [🎃] Fake an Egg

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10769384180, by Slime Time Studios (Group), launched Oct 4.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10769384180.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*
- *It's the closest new game to Hide as a Painting: a hide-in-plain-sight game inside the steal-a-pet format.*

**Core loop:** disguise yourself as an egg, wait for a victim, steal their best pet while they aren't looking, and hatch your own pets to collect.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- "Not every egg is what it seems… 🥚👀"
- Two instructions: "1. Disguise as an egg and wait for a victim! 2. Steal their best pet while they're not looking!"
- You play the deceiver from the very first moment.

## Retention engine
- **Pet collection:** hatching, plus +1 pet-slot passes.
- **Revenge-stealing** between players.
- **A counter-scan system:** the "Scan Jam" passes imply players can scan for fakes, and fakers can jam the scan.
- **Not visible:** no trading or offline income in the metadata.

## Co-play
Servers hold 10 (RoWatcher shows 95% average fill). The game is player-against-player, so strangers fill it; friends aren't needed.

## Monetization
- **8 game passes, all created Oct 25–26:**
  - +1 Pet Slot in 3 tiers: 99, 149, 199 R$;
  - Scan Jam in 3 tiers: 129, 229, 329 R$;

- **Watch-out:** Scan Jam is paid power over other players.

## What we'd steal
- **The disguise *is* the theft (Hide as a Painting).**
  - In Fake an Egg, players hide as an object to ambush others. In Hide as a Painting, hiders pose as paintings to steal or guard collectible frames.
  - That plugs the hide mechanic straight into the v2 collection economy.
- **Scanning and jamming as the seeker/hider tug-of-war (Hide as a Painting).**
  - Give seekers a scan with a cooldown, and give hiders a jam they earn in play.
  - Sell only cosmetic versions. Don't copy the paid Scan Jam, because the economy plan rules out paying to win.
- **A countdown in the title before each update (Night-Shift Museum).** "[30 MIN]" sets up an appointment. Use it for every weekly wing drop.
