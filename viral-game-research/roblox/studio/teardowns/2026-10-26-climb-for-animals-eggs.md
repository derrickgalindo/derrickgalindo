# Teardown: [🎃] Climb For Animals Eggs 🥚

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10765490774, by 100% GRIND (Group), launched Sep 6.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10765490774.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*

**Core loop:** climb to higher biomes, find and hatch eggs, and place the animals at your base for cash; spend it on speed and upgrades, then rebirth and climb higher.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- Train on the treadmill for speed.
- Climb to your first eggs and hatch an animal.
- Place it at your base, and cash starts.

## Retention engine
- **Offline progress:** "Your Animals keep earning Cash while you're offline!"
- **Rebirth** multiplies earnings.
- **Biome progression:** higher climbs reach new biomes.
- **Base expansion** to hold more animals.
- **A reward for joining the group:** "+10K Speed".
- **Not present:** trading and ranked play.

## Co-play
Servers hold 5 and are always full (RoWatcher shows 100% average fill). It's mostly solo play next to others.

## Monetization
- **5 game passes, added over 3 weeks:**
  - 2x Cash, 99 R$, and 2x Egg Growth, 229 R$ (Sep 20);
  - VIP, 150 R$ (Oct 5);
  - Auto Hatch, 29 R$ (Oct 7);
  - JetPack, 69 R$ (Oct 8).
- **Revenue: about $16.3k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $12.2k–20.4k).
