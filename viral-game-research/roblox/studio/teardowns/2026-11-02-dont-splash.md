- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10771421858.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*

**Core loop:** in a party of 1–4, cross flooded Poolrooms by jumping between platforms without splashing, because a blind monster hunts by sound. Each run earns coins for tools.

## First 60 seconds (per the guide)
- You spawn in the first pool room. The rule is in the title: don't splash.
- A bad landing makes noise and the monster turns toward you. The first lesson comes from failing.
- Tools are the answer:
  - pebbles to throw as a distraction;
  - a flare that stuns the monster for about 5 seconds;
  - a rope a teammate can use to pull a downed player back.

## Retention engine
- **Progression:** coins buy tools and upgrades. Easy mode pays about half of Normal (per the guide).
- **Difficulty ladder:**
  - Easy has 1 monster and Normal has 3;
  - Hard has up to 5 and unlocks after beating Normal twice (per the guide).
- **Content drops:** 4 rooms at launch, then **9 after the Oct 24 update**, when the title carried "[NEW STAGES]" (guide; our Oct 26 title snapshot matches).
- **Not present:** collection, trading or offline progress.

## Co-play
- Built for small parties: the rope rescue needs a teammate.
- 10 players per server, 88% average fill (RoWatcher).

## Monetization
- **12 priced game passes:**
  - at launch (Oct 17): 2x Cash (99 R$), 4th/5th Tool Slot (99/149), Rescue Rope (30), and "Max ___" upgrades for each tool (75 each);
  - All Upgrades Maxed (149), on Oct 24, the same day as the new stages;
  - Fall Pack (150), on Oct 28;
  - a Halloween Pack, shown in the API without a price (the guide says 79 R$).
- **Revenue: about $30.1k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $22.6k–37.6k), the highest among this week's new games.

## Update cadence

## What we'd steal
- **Noise as the threat (Night-Shift Museum).** Make one anomaly react to sound: creaky parquet, a dropped keyring, running. It's cheap to build and easy to watch, since a viewer can hear the mistake.
- **A rescue tool that only works with a friend (both games).** The rope pulls a downed player back. Give the guard a radio or a "drag to safety" action, and let hiders free a "caught" teammate in Hide as a Painting.
- **Cheap "max this tool" passes (Night-Shift Museum).** At 75 R$ per tool, the passes match the tools in the game, which is a simple first monetization plan for the learning game.
