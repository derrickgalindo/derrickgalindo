## What we'd steal
- **Time the second content drop before the peak, not after (Night-Shift Museum).** Sell Fries' chapter came about 2 weeks after its day-26 peak, and its effect lasted hours. Plan Night-Shift Museum's update #2 for days ~14–21, while the recommender is still expanding. This is one data point, so treat it as a hypothesis.
- **Tell players what the chapter is (Night-Shift Museum).** "[CH2]" with an unchanged description gives a returning player no reason to come back. Put the new wing or new anomaly in the title and the first line of the description.
