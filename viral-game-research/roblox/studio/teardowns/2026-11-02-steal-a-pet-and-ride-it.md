# Teardown: [🎃] Steal A Pet And Ride It!

*Nov 2 2026.*
- *Universe 10769315427, by Snail Riders (Group), launched Oct 4.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10769315427.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*
- *Skipped last week because RoWatcher had only started tracking it on Oct 25.*

**Core loop:** steal cages from sleeping bosses, run home before a boss catches you, open the cages for rare pets, and ride those pets to run faster, while the pets earn cash in your barn.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- Walk up to a sleeping boss and grab a cage.
- Run home while the boss chases you. The chase is the hook.
- Open the cage, get a pet, ride it. Being faster right away makes the next steal easier.

## Retention engine
- **Collection:**
  - World 2 adds "10 new biomes, 80 new pets", across 20 biomes in 2 worlds;
  - a limited Dinosaur Era Cage with 12 limited pets;
  - meteor showers that drop rare cages.
- **Progression:** treadmill speed, barn upgrades, and a rebirth that keeps your pets.
- **Light PvP:** "Lasso thieves and drop banana peels".
- **Not advertised:** trading or offline earnings.

## Co-play
- Optional, with a reward: "Play with friends to earn bonus cash!"
- 7 players per server, 100% average fill (RoWatcher).

## Monetization
- **13 priced game passes, added about weekly alongside content:**
  - Oct 8: 3x Open Speed (299 R$), Auto Collect (49);
  - Oct 16: VIP (199), 3 barn skins (99–149);
  - Oct 25: Halloween Trail, Treadmill and Aura, and 2x Candy (199–249);
  - Oct 30: Mastery Pass (399), Extra Boss Drop (199), Boss Luck Boost (199).
  - There's also an "OP Pet Pack" with no price shown.
- **Revenue: about $13.0k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $9.7k–16.2k).

- A free Legendary pet for joining the community group.

## What we'd steal
- **Steal from the NPC, not from other players (Night-Shift Museum).** Stealing from a sleeping boss works solo and avoids griefing. Invert it for the museum: the anomaly "sleeps" in a gallery, and the guard has to recover a stolen exhibit without waking it.
- **A cash bonus for playing with friends (both games).** It's one line in the description and it nudges intentional co-play, which the recommender counts.
- **Limited-time items in the collection (Hide as a Painting).** The limited Dinosaur cage with 12 pets is a model for event-only frames and paints that stay tradeable after the event ends.
