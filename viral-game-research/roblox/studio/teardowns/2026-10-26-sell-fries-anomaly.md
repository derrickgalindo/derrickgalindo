# Teardown: 🎃 Sell Fries! [Anomaly] 🍟

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10767380385, by Pharting (Group), launched Sep 21.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10767380385.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds are pieced together from the game description and a third-party guide ([Of Zen and Computing, Oct 16](https://www.ofzenandcomputing.com/all-sell-fries-anomalies-guide-how-to-survive-every-monster/)).*

**Core loop:** collect potatoes, make fries, serve customers and upgrade the kitchen; at night, deal with the anomalies or die.

## First 60 seconds
- You spawn at a fry stand during the day. The description's 4 steps are: collect potatoes from the pile, make fries, serve customers, upgrade the kitchen.
- Cash comes in right away. There's no menu between you and play.
- The threat is announced but held back: "But be careful when Night falls…"
- Per the guide, anomalies arrive on a short timer after dark:
  - from Night 1: a face at the window (close the shutter with the red Emergency button) and a Potato Eater (hold E on the pile);
  - later nights: harder ones, up to "The Giant" from Night 5.

## Retention engine
- **Progression:** kitchen upgrades, plus a defense unlock: per the guide, a "Fry Shooter" at 12,000 cash.
- **Escalation:** the anomaly roster grows night by night.
- **Not present:** trading, collection, offline progress or ranked play.

## Co-play

## What we'd steal
- **Day economy, night threat (Night-Shift Museum).**
  - Give the guard a daytime job that pays: restoring and hanging paintings, which earns cash for upgrades.
  - Night anomalies then threaten what you've built. This gives the learning game the progression engine the pitch scores at 0.
- **One big panic button per anomaly (Night-Shift Museum).**
  - Sell Fries teaches each anomaly with a single physical response: a shutter button, or holding E on the pile.
  - Do the same for the museum: one obvious action per anomaly, which is good for phones.
- **A defense you unlock with cash (Night-Shift Museum).** Sell Fries' 12,000-cash Fry Shooter is a goal to save toward. A museum equivalent: an alarmed laser grid or a UV floodlight.
