# Teardown: [🎃] Bike For Egg!

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10766620257, by Huron Games (Group), launched Sep 15.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10766620257.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*

**Core loop:** train speed on a treadmill, then bike out to steal eggs from pets and from other players; hatch them for rare pets that earn cash, and upgrade your treadmill and base.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- Get on the treadmill for speed.
- Make your first egg steal from a pet.
- Hatch it, and the pet starts earning money.

## Retention engine
- **Collection with rarity layers:** "rarer eggs, pets, sizes, and mutations".
- **An Animal Index** with rewards to claim.
- **PvP egg theft.**
- **Not visible:** trading, offline income or ranked play.

## Co-play
Servers hold 7 (RoWatcher shows 95% average fill). It's played among strangers, so friends aren't needed.

## Monetization
- **2 game passes, both 99 R$:** X2 Money and X2 Growth, both added Sep 22.
- **Revenue: about $12.8k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $9.6k–15.9k).

## Update cadence

- The RoWatcher change log is empty.

## What we'd steal
- **Mutations and sizes as a cheap rarity layer (Hide as a Painting).**
  - Each frame or paint can roll a size or "mutation" (gilded, cracked, glowing).
  - That multiplies collectible variety without new art, which is what keeps Fisch and Fish It going.
- **Ship the update on Thursday or Friday, before the weekend (both games).** Bike For Egg's best-ever sample came about 11 hours after a Thursday-night publish.
