**Core loop:** each round, one random player types a word, the word becomes a hazard on the island (a tsunami, a T-Rex, a nuke, tacos), hazards stack up, and the last player alive wins.

## First 60 seconds
- The pitch fits in the first line: "Type ANY word. It happens."
- You spawn on an island with up to 39 others.
- Within one round you either watch a stranger's word wreck the map, or you're the one who gets to type.
- Each round shows a cause and its effect, which makes it easy to clip.

## Retention engine
- **Collection:** guides describe an in-game Index that logs the words you've found, and only some words trigger named effects ([bloxodes wiki](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/survive-your-ideas/words), [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/survive-your-ideas-command-words-roblox/)). "Find a word nobody has tried" is the discovery hook.
- **Currency:** surviving earns Cash, which buys Meta Words. Codes give lucky spins ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/survive-your-ideas-codes)).
- **Not visible:** trading, offline progress or ranked play.
- **Engagement:**
  - like ratio 0.89;
  - **only ~7.6 minutes per visit**, but 10.9M visits/day;
  - short, repeatable rounds.

## Co-play
- 40-player servers at 70% average fill.
- It needs a crowd but not friends. Strangers are both the hazard writers and the audience.

## What we'd steal
- **(Night-Shift Museum) Let one player author the round.** Each shift, one random guard secretly picks which anomaly appears (from an unlocked list), and the others must spot it. That turns a solo puzzle into co-play for free, and the person picking is the one who'll want to clip it.
- **(both) An Index of discovered items.** "You found 23 of 80 anomalies" in Night-Shift Museum, and "23 of 80 poses or paintings" in Hide as a Painting. That's a discovery-collection retention engine that costs only content.
- **(Hide as a Painting) Sell authorship, not power.** Paying to choose the next gallery theme is a "meta word" that doesn't break fairness.
