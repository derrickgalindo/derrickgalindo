# Teardown: Raise The Biggest Family

*Nov 2 2026.*
- *Universe 10770699460, by Novkod X Level0Expert (Group), launched Oct 13.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10770699460.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*

**Core loop:** adopt children and feed, bathe and care for them as they grow. They **age while you're offline**, so you come back daily.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
A short story-driven onboarding with 4 steps, each with a clear objective:
1. get your approval letter;
2. drive to the adoption center;
3. pass your interview;
4. bring your kid home.

## Retention engine
- **Offline progress is the headline.** The first line of the description is "Your Children AGE OFFLINE!!! come back daily to give them love!"
- **Collection or progression:** a bigger family, bigger houses and more vehicles.
- **Not advertised:** trading or ranked play.

## Co-play
- Social roleplay at 18 per server, with 100% average fill (RoWatcher).
- Friends aren't required.

## Monetization

  - Oct 27: Starter/Pro/Ultimate packs, VIP, 2x Coins and Auto Collect.
- **Revenue: about $5.8k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $4.4k–7.3k).

## What we'd steal
- **Put the reason to come back in the first line (Hide as a Painting).** Pick one offline timer and put it at the top of the description, for example: "Your paintings dry overnight. Come back to frame them." Then make it real in the loop.
- **Onboarding as a short story with 4 steps (Night-Shift Museum).** Something like: hired letter → collect your badge → first patrol → first anomaly. Each step is one clear objective, which helps keep players past the 60-second bounce window.
