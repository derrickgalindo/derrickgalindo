# Teardown: Hide to Steal Eggs

*Nov 2 2026.*
- *Universe 10769562933, by Crazay Future (Group), launched Oct 6.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10769562933.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*
- *It's the second 10k+ game built on Fake an Egg's idea ([Oct 26 teardown](2026-10-26-fake-an-egg.md)): disguise yourself as the thing being stolen.*

**Core loop:** hide inside an egg to sneak onto other players' plots, steal eggs back to your own plot, and hatch them into rare pets that earn money. Training Strength lets you carry heavier, rarer eggs.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- "🫣 Hide inside eggs to sneak in people's plots". The disguise is the move you make on your first steal.
- Carry an egg home and hatch it to get a pet that earns money.
- The Strength bar shows the first upgrade goal: heavier eggs.

## Retention engine
- **Collection:** rare pets from hatching, and "rarer animals and heavier eggs".
- **Progression:** Strength training.
- **Not advertised:** trading, offline earnings or ranked play.

## Co-play
- Other players are both targets and threats, since you steal from their plots. Friends aren't needed.
- 10 players per server, 82% average fill (RoWatcher).

## Monetization
- **3 game passes, all added Oct 25,** on day 19, when it was at about 2.6k concurrent: x2 Power (9 R$), x2 Money (120 R$) and x2 Egg Hatch Speed (149 R$). It launched with no passes and added them once it had players.
- **Revenue: about $1.3k/day.** That's RoWatcher's model estimate (range $0.6k–1.9k).

## What we'd steal
- **Give hiders a job (Hide as a Painting).** Hiding here is how you steal, not just how you survive. A hider frozen as a painting should be able to do something risky, like swapping a real masterpiece for a fake while the seeker isn't looking. That keeps hiders busy and makes rounds watchable.
- **Launch without passes and add them after traction (Night-Shift Museum).** This game and Airport Security (first passes on Oct 28) both grew before they sold anything. For the learning game, ship with 0–1 passes and add more once the D1 numbers are in.
