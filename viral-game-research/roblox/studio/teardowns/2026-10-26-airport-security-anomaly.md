**Core loop:** on the night shift, inspect each passenger with several tools, then let them board or deny them; let a monster through and it hunts you.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- "You've been hired as airport security for the night shift."

- Then you decide: deny anyone who seems off, "but don't send away the innocent".

- **Fear instead of health:** "No health bar. Get too scared and you crash out."

## What we'd steal
- **Several inspection tools per item (Night-Shift Museum).**
  - Swap the pitch's single LOCK OUT button for 3–4 cheap checks: a UV lamp, a crate X-ray, the provenance papers, a camera feed.
  - Each tool catches different anomalies, and with 2–4 guards the tools can be split between players.
- **A penalty for wrongly turning away the innocent (Night-Shift Museum).** Airport Security leads with "don't send away the innocent". This keeps the judgement call from collapsing into "deny everything".
- **A recurring anomaly that remembers you (Night-Shift Museum, then Hide as a Painting).**
  - A portrait that reacts to how you treated it last night gives players a reason to come back that costs no new content.
  - The same idea carries over to the museum event in Hide as a Painting.
