# Teardown: Clone Baby Care (Anomaly)

*Oct 26 2026.*
- *Universe 10770018231, by Oddbaby Studio (Group), launched Oct 9.*
- *Data: `data/roblox_games.csv`, RoWatcher history `data/raw/rowatcher/10770018231.json`, and the Roblox game-passes API.*
- *I haven't played it. The first 60 seconds come from the game description.*

**Core loop:** over one night, feed, bathe and put five clone babies to bed while strange things happen in the house and something in the dark comes for them.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- "One night, five babies came to your house."
- You get the care tasks straight away: feed, bathe, put to bed.
- The hook: "strange things begin to happen… 'something' in the dark is after the babies, too. Protect the babies…"

## Retention engine
- **Protecting someone instead of surviving yourself.** Five babies you've cared for are at stake, which is an emotional hook.
- **Not visible:** no collection, trading, offline progress or ranked play shows in the metadata.

## Co-play
Servers hold 24, but only about half fill (RoWatcher shows 52% average fill). The metadata doesn't say whether friends are needed.

## What we'd steal
- **Something to protect, not just something to reject (Night-Shift Museum).**
  - Put a few "star paintings" in the gallery that the anomalies try to swap out or carry off.
  - The guard is defending something they recognize, not only working through a queue.
- **Care tasks as the calm phase (Night-Shift Museum).** Feed, bathe and bed is simple, readable busywork between scares. For the museum: dust frames, adjust the lights, log visitors.
