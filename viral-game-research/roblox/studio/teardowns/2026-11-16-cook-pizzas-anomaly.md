**Core loop:** make pizzas and serve customers, spot the ones who "aren't human", and survive the lights-out phase when "it" hunts you.

## First 60 seconds (from the description)
- "Make the pizzas. Serve the customers." The job is the tutorial: a familiar task straight away.
- The twist is announced up front: "some customers aren't human… when the lights go out, don't let it see you."
- The description is 4 lines long. All the selling is done by the thumbnail and the title tag.

- **Combat or escape gear:** Golden Minigun, Fast Legs and a Revive Pass. So the lights-out phase has a fight-or-flee layer, unlike pure spot-the-difference Anomaly games.

## What we'd steal
- **(Night-Shift Museum) Pair the anomaly-spotting with a "lights out" chase.** The Anomaly games that are holding up have a second phase, and this one even sells a revive. At closing time, the museum's lights go out and a misplaced exhibit hunts the guard.
- **(Night-Shift Museum) Limited-time classes as the seasonal hook.** Penguin and Gingerbread classes ship just ahead of the holidays. Seasonal guard uniforms (Winter Guard, Ghost Guard) are cheap to make and reusable as Hide as a Painting cosmetics.
