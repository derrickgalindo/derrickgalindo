**Core loop:** spawn dummies and hurt them with weapons, syringes, cars and world events, then watch them react physically. It's solo, with no win state.

## First 60 seconds
- "Every dummy is a real physics body. Hit it, throw it, blow it up."
- From the first click, the dummies "stumble, beg, fight back," and limbs come off.
- The content list does the selling:
  - 23 weapons, up to a black hole;
  - 14 syringes (zombies, balloon ragdolls);
  - cars to crash;
  - NPC wars;
  - world events (Nuke, Zero-G, Flash Freeze);
  - a sandbox with slow-motion and rewind.
