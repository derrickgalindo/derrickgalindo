**Core loop:** queue into a 4v4 soccer match where every player is a physics ragdoll. Nothing is scripted, so the comedy comes from flailing bodies, and you queue again.

## First 60 seconds
- The code "WELCOME" sits at the top of the description, so there's a free reward on your first visit.
- 4v4 quick play drops you straight into a match.
- The ragdoll movement is the joke, and it's visible the moment you try to kick.

## Retention engine
- **Modes, not collection.** On Nov 10 the description added:
  - 8v8 pro servers "with referees, penalties, free kicks, throw-ins, VAR, and offsides";
  - a Clubs mode to queue 4v4 with friends;
  - practice mode.
- **Not visible:** collection, trading, offline progress or ranked play.
- **Engagement:** like ratio 0.73 (weak), ~11 minutes per visit. The description still apologizes that the game is "in its early stages of development".
